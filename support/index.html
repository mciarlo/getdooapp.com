Creating reminders ↑

Write a reminder the way you’d say it. Quick add reads the date, time and repeat from your words and highlights them as you type.

  • Soccer practice every Friday at 3pm
  • Dentist Thursday at 9
  • Groceries. bananas, milk, eggs

Text after a period becomes a note or a checklist. The last example creates a Groceries card with three items.

Prefer to set everything yourself? Turn off Use quick add in Settings. On Mac, ⇧⌘A turns quick add on or off.

You can also add reminders with Siri, from the share sheet in other apps with Add to Doo, or from the URL scheme.

Working with cards ↑

Doo shows what’s due as a stack of cards. Keep each card to a single step. Groceries, appointments, first drafts. This helps you start and complete items without excuses.

Complete

Finish the top card to clear it from the stack. Changed your mind? Undo brings it back.

Snooze

Snooze moves a card out of the way for now. Pick how long a snooze lasts with Default snooze in Settings. On Mac, the snooze menu also offers 15 minutes, 1 hour and until tonight.

Checklists

Check off items inside a card as you go. Use Convert list to cards to turn each checklist item into its own reminder.

Lists ↑

Use lists to group cards by project or theme. Term papers. Yard renovations. App launches. Lists are meant to be short-lived. Make one for a project and let it go when the work is done.

Create a list from the list view, with Siri, or on iPad and Mac with ⇧⌘N.

Someday ↑

Someday is a list for ideas, aspirations and nice-to-haves. Things you save there stay out of your stack until you’re ready for them.

Reminders saved to Someday don’t have dates or notifications. When you’re ready, move a card out of Someday and give it a date.

Calendars ↑

Turn on Connect to Calendar in Settings to show your events alongside reminders in the list view. Choose how far ahead to show with Events range.

Actions

  • Move to Doo turns one of your own events into a reminder and removes it from your calendar.
  • Copy to Doo makes a reminder from an event and leaves the event where it is. Use it for invitations from other people.

If an event can’t be turned into a reminder, for example because of a repeat schedule Doo doesn’t support, Doo tells you and leaves the event unchanged.

Notifications ↑

Each reminder can notify you when it’s due, or earlier, such as 1 hour before. Choose a custom sound with Notification sound in Settings.

Nag notifications

Turn on Nag notifications for a reminder you can’t miss. Doo sends three extra notifications, ten minutes apart.

Reminders without notifications

Turn off Notifications on a reminder to keep it in your stack without an alert.

Locations ↑

Add a location to get reminded when you arrive or depart somewhere. Set a distance to choose how close is close enough.

For quick access to Home and Work, pick a contact card. Doo will use the home or work address for the selected contact. If an address doesn’t show up, make sure the contact card includes one.

Sharing ↑

Share reminders with friends and family. Bring up the reminder options and tap Add people to invite them. When someone completes a shared task, everyone gets a notification.

Sharing uses iCloud, so everyone needs to be signed in to their Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, and iCloud syncing turned on in Doo.

New shared tasks you join show up under All.

Siri and Shortcuts ↑

Doo’s actions are available in the Shortcuts app and work with Siri. Try saying:

  • “Add a reminder in Doo”
  • “Dictate a reminder in Doo”
  • “Describe a reminder in Doo”
  • “Complete a reminder in Doo”
  • “Create a new list in Doo”

Dictate and Describe use quick add, so you can say the whole thing at once, like “Call Mom Sunday at 3.”

Widgets ↑

Doo supports Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, and watch complications. Use widgets to see what’s next or waiting for completion. Tap a widget to open Doo to the selected list.

iCloud and your data ↑

Doo syncs your reminders across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch with iCloud. Turn on iCloud syncing from Settings in Doo. You need to be signed in to your Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on.

To take a copy of your reminders with you, use Export your data under Data in Settings.

URL scheme ↑

Create reminders from other apps and automations with Doo’s URL scheme. See the URL scheme reference for parameters and examples.

FAQ ↑

Why does Doo want reminders to be a single step?

Small steps are easier to start and easier to finish. Big things are made of small things, so give yourself room to work toward the larger whole and celebrate each step along the way.

I moved an event to Doo, but it’s still in my calendar.

Doo only removes an event once it can recreate it exactly as a reminder. If the event has a repeat schedule Doo doesn’t support, the event is left unchanged.

Why doesn’t my Someday reminder have a date?

Someday is for things without a deadline, so dates and notifications are removed when you save there. Move the card out of Someday to schedule it.

Someone joined my shared reminder but can’t see it.

Check that they’re signed in to their Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, and that iCloud syncing is on in Doo.
