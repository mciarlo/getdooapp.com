Philosophy

The best tools are nearly invisible.

Doo is everything you need and nothing you don’t. Intention in every corner.

No busywork allowed.

Lists are short-lived. Reminders are kept to a single step. Guided by a universal truth. Big things are made of small things.

Do big things.

  • Cards

    Keep them small. Groceries. Appointments. First drafts. Give yourself room to work towards a larger whole. Set yourself up for consistent progress.

  • Lists

    Use lists to group cards by project or theme. Term papers. Yard renovations. App launches. Celebrate each step, not just the last.

  • Someday

    Save ideas, aspirations, and nice to haves in a list made for the future. Keep your head clear. Return to things on your own time.
