About

Made in 2016. Used every day since.

I’m Mike. I’ve designed and built Doo myself since 2016, because I was tired of apps glorifying busywork and calling it productivity.

Why it works the way it does

Big things are made of small things. This isn’t just a tagline, it’s how the most productive people I know get things done. They just start. Break things up into small pieces you can act on without excuses. Use lists and cards to sift through the noise and give yourself clarity.

Everything else is left out on purpose. I don’t want to build an app that becomes a chore. This is my benchmark when I think about a feature. If it turns planning into busywork, it doesn't ship.

Recognition

Doo has been covered by Wired, BuzzFeed, The Next Web, Lifehacker and Macworld. The app has received Webby award nominations for design.
