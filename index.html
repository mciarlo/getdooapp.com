    Features

    • Just type

      Write it the way you’d say it. Doo picks out the date, time and repeat.

      Soccer practice Fri 3pm

    • Lists and notes

      Add a checklist after a period, like “Groceries. bananas, milk, eggs.”

      Groceries bananas milk eggs

    • Bring in your calendars

      Move your events into Doo, or copy the ones you’re invited to.

      Thanksgiving dinner · Thu 4 PM
      Move to Doo
      Copy to Doo

    • Nag notifications

      For the things you can’t miss, Doo sends three extra notifications, ten minutes apart.

      Doonow
      Call Mom re: holiday

    • Someday

      Park the things you’re not ready for. They stay out of the way until you are.

      Someday
      Sign up for pottery class
      Fix the leaky faucet

    • Share with the people you live with

      Shared lists for chores, groceries and plans.

    • Widgets

      See what’s next right from your Home Screen.

      Thursday at 9

    • Siri and Shortcuts

      Add tasks by voice or from your own shortcuts.

    • iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch
