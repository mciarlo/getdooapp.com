Features

Focus on one thing at a time

Don’t let anxiety and stress give you an excuse to procrastinate. Pick one or two things and snooze the rest. Consistency creates progress.

Group reminders by project or goal

Organize your plans. Keep reminders short and actionable to feel your progress each day.

Save things for when you’re ready

Use the “Someday” list to get things off your mind.

Just type to create

Create a reminder how you’d say it, like “Call mom Friday at 3.” Supports dates, weekdays, end dates, repeats, notes, and checklists.

Bring in your calendars for a unified schedule

Connect your iOS or Mac calendar to move events into Doo or view them alongside your reminders.
